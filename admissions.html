Admissions

Begin Your Journey With Us

Welcome to Fiction International School! We are happy to welcome students who are eager to learn, explore and grow. Our admission process is designed to help parents understand our school and the opportunities we provide.

Admission Process

Step 1 – Enquiry

Parents can contact the school to enquire about admission details.

Step 2 – Application

Collect and complete the admission application form.

Step 3 – Document Submission

Submit the required student documents to the school office.

Step 4 – Admission Confirmation

Contact the school office for confirmation and further instructions.

Documents Required

Keep These Documents Ready

• Student Birth Certificate
• Previous School Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
• Previous Academic Records (if applicable)
• Passport Size Photographs
• Parent or Guardian Contact Details
